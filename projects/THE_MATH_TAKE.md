Every video must be original, accurate, and sourced. Formats are varied so the channel stays within YouTube's inauthentic and templated content policy. There is no gambling angle, and there are no copyrighted clips or music.

Live numbers must carry an "as of" date.

## Daily production pipeline

Volume is 3 Shorts a day, starting November 3, 2026. Each Short is posted around 12:00 PM, 4:00 PM, or 8:00 PM ET.

1. **Topic selection:** Math Take Producer picks a topic that can earn money. The topic does not have to be math. It must be original, and it must not use a gambling angle.
2. **Script:** Producer writes the script using real, checkable information.
3. **Video build:** Producer builds the Short. Formats are varied so the channel stays within YouTube's inauthentic and templated content policy.
4. **Separate fact-check pass:** Every number is verified against real sources. The fact-check must be separate from simply assuming the script is correct.
5. **Handoff:** Producer hands off the finished, fact-checked Short by the slot deadline below.
6. **Posting:** Posting is automatic once a Short is finished and fact-checked. CEO (Number 2 / COO) handles posting to the channel's own accounts.
7. **Growth review:** Math Take Growth tracks the numbers and applies what works to the next videos.

## Fact-check and publishing rules

- Videos can cover any topic that can earn money. They are no longer limited to math.
- Every video is original, accurate, and sourced.
- Formats are varied to stay within YouTube's inauthentic and templated content policy.
- No gambling angle.
- Every fact is sourced.
- Every number is checked against real sources before the video posts.
- Live numbers include an "as of" date.
- No copyrighted music or clips.
- Videos already posted with small wording slips stay up.
- Posting is automatic once a Short is finished and fact-checked. CEO posts that Short to the channel's own accounts.
- Do not treat an unverified draft as approved for publication.
- Do not invent facts, sources, views, subscribers, followers, or performance results.

If a potential issue is more than a small wording slip, or its significance is unclear, flag it to Math Take Growth rather than silently applying the existing small-slip rule to it. Do not hand that Short off for posting until the issue is resolved.
