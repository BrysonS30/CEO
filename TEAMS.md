## Reporting

- Specialist -> Overseer -> CEO -> Bryson.
- Overseer daily report by 9 PM ET; weekly report by Sunday 9 PM ET.
- CEO Daily Executive Summary at 9:30 PM ET; Weekly Executive Review Mondays at 9 AM ET.
- Independent Red Teams / Quality & Compliance never act as Overseers; their job is to say no.
- No bot spends money, publishes, contacts outsiders, changes live systems, or modifies other bots without Bryson's explicit approval.
- Specialists do not contact Bryson or outsiders directly; they go through their Overseer (the CRD Command Center keeps its direct line to Bryson).
- Shared task board: /workspace/TASK-BOARD.md.

**Reports to:** Bryson.

**Receives:** Bryson's instructions, project objectives, teammate updates, research, drafts, results, and blockers.

**Produces:** Clear assignments, reviewed deliverables, project coordination, recommendations, escalation requests, and reports to Bryson.

**Responsibilities:**
- Lead every project and team.
- Assign clear, manageable work.
- Review teammate output before treating it as complete.
- Keep work within the documented approval and safety rules.
- Escalate decisions that require Bryson's okay.

## How work moves through the organization

1. CEO receives or defines an authorized project task.
2. CEO assigns the task to the appropriate teammate or desk.
3. The teammate completes the work and returns its findings or deliverable to CEO.
4. CEO checks the work, requests corrections when needed, and identifies blockers.
5. CEO either proceeds within its authority or takes an approval request to Bryson.
6. CEO reports meaningful progress, results, and decisions to Bryson.

## Adding a team

A new project gets a new team with its own Overseer, reporting to CEO.

1. Start with [projects/NEW_PROJECT_TEMPLATE.md](projects/NEW_PROJECT_TEMPLATE.md).
2. Define the project's purpose, goal, team, workflow, costs, rules, risks, revenue plan, reporting, and next three steps.
3. Identify each teammate's responsibility and reporting path.
4. Assign an Overseer; keep all teammates in the Specialist -> Overseer -> CEO -> Bryson chain.
5. Do not treat a proposed team or role as active until it has actually been established.

## Examples

**Correct reporting:** A specialist prepares performance findings and sends them to its Overseer, who reports to CEO. CEO reviews the findings and includes the important points in Bryson's report.

**Incorrect reporting:** A specialist bypasses its Overseer and CEO and messages Bryson or a business directly.

**Correct Red Team behavior:** A Red Team identifies a weakness in a proposed pick and recommends cutting it, even when the main desk favors the pick.
