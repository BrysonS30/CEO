# The Math Take

> **Purpose:** This file documents The Math Take's content mission, publishing platforms, daily production pipeline, fact-check requirements, team responsibilities, and path to income.

## What it is

Short videos about the real math behind sports, money, crypto and everyday life.

The purpose is to explain the math behind topics in a clear, factual short-video format. Every fact must be sourced; live numbers must carry an "as of" date.

## Team

### Math Take Producer

**Responsibilities:** Scripts, fact-checks, and builds the Shorts.

**Inputs:** Topic ideas and real source material.

**Outputs:** Topic selection, script, fact-check material, and completed Short for CEO review.

### Math Take Growth

**Responsibilities:** Stats, topics, newsletter, and honest monetization.

**Inputs:** Available performance numbers, topic ideas, and newsletter activity.

**Outputs:** Performance tracking, growth observations, topic recommendations, newsletter work, and honest monetization work.

Both roles report to CEO. CEO reviews the completed work before it is treated as ready to post.

## Daily production pipeline

1. **Topic selection:** Math Take Producer picks a topic.
2. **Script:** Producer writes the script using real, checkable information.
3. **Video build:** Producer builds the Short.
4. **Separate fact-check pass:** Every number is verified against real sources. The fact-check must be separate from simply assuming the script is correct.
5. **CEO review:** CEO reviews the finished, fact-checked Short.
6. **Posting:** CEO posts the finished, fact-checked Short to all of the channel's own accounts under Bryson's standing okay.
7. **Growth review:** Math Take Growth tracks the numbers and applies what works to the next videos.

### Timing and handoffs

The reporting schedule records the Short as made, fact-checked, and posted at approximately **4:00 PM daily**. No separate clock times for the individual production handoffs are specified in the current project plan. Do not invent additional deadlines or claim a handoff time has been agreed.

## Fact-check and publishing rules

- Every fact is sourced.
- Every number is checked against real sources before the video posts.
- Live numbers include an "as of" date.
- No copyrighted music or clips.
- Videos already posted with small wording slips stay up.
- CEO posts only finished, fact-checked Shorts to the channel's own accounts under the standing okay.
- Do not treat an unverified draft as approved for publication.
- Do not invent facts, sources, views, subscribers, or performance results.

If a potential issue is more than a small wording slip, or its significance is unclear, flag it to CEO rather than silently applying the existing small-slip rule to it.

## Examples

**Correct:** Producer creates a Short, a separate fact-check pass verifies its numbers and sources, CEO reviews it, and CEO posts it to the channel's own accounts. Growth records actual performance data for future work.

**Incorrect:** A Short is posted before fact-checking, a live number has no "as of" date, or projected views are reported as actual views.

**Correct handling of a small wording slip:** A video already posted with a small wording slip stays up, consistent with the existing rule. Do not extend that rule to a material factual error without CEO review.
