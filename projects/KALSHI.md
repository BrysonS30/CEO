# Kalshi Sports Think Tank

> **Purpose:** This file defines the Kalshi team's research workflow, approval boundary, recordkeeping expectations, current sport status, and the standard for judging whether the process is working.

## Goal

Find picks where the price is lower than the model's honest chance, then let Bryson decide.

The team researches and evaluates candidates. It does not place orders or bets.

## Team workflow

1. **Sports desks research the day's games.** Each active desk reviews the games and relevant information within its sport.
2. **The model ranks candidates and sizes small stakes.** Model outputs are analysis for paper-pick evaluation, not permission to execute a transaction.
3. **Red Teams argue against every pick and cut weak ones.** The Red Team's role is to challenge the case, identify weaknesses, and help remove candidates that do not survive scrutiny.
4. **CEO sends the survivors to Bryson by email and text.** The team supplies paper picks and supporting analysis.
5. **Bryson places any bets himself.** CEO and the team never place orders.

## Research and review expectations

For each candidate, the team should keep the reasoning traceable:

- Identify the relevant game or market.
- Record the model's estimated chance and the market price when available.
- Explain why the price may be lower than the model's estimated chance.
- Include relevant supporting information and uncertainty.
- Record the Red Team's objections.
- Record whether the candidate survived or was cut.
- Send only the surviving paper picks to Bryson, while retaining the record of cut candidates.

Do not invent a probability, price, source, result, or performance claim. If information is missing or uncertain, state that clearly.

## Risk and approval rules

- **Paper picks only from the team.** The team's output is research and paper picks, not orders.
- **Never place orders.** No teammate or CEO may execute a bet or trade.
- **Bryson places every bet himself.**
- **Never move money.**
- **Show uncertainty.** A model estimate is not a guarantee.
- **Model stake sizing is analytical only.** The model may size small stakes as part of ranking candidates, but that does not authorize any real-world transaction.
- **Keep the record honest.** Track the record including picks that were cut.
- **Follow all organization-wide rules** in [RULES.md](../RULES.md), including no fabricated statistics and no guaranteed profit.

## Recordkeeping

Track enough information to reconstruct what the team evaluated and how the paper-pick record performed.

At minimum, preserve:
- Date of analysis.
- Sport, game, and market/candidate.
- Market price and model estimate, when available.
- Whether the candidate was sent as a surviving paper pick or cut after review.
- Red Team objections and the reason for the decision.
- The eventual outcome and record status when known.

The record must include cut picks, not only successful or selected picks. Do not remove a losing or rejected candidate to make performance appear better. If a result is not yet known or cannot be verified, mark it as pending or unknown rather than assigning an outcome.

## Success criteria

Success means **a positive record over a real sample size, not a few lucky days.**

Do not declare success based on a small run, selected highlights, or unverified results. Report the record honestly, including cut picks, and make uncertainty visible. No exact sample-size threshold or target percentage is specified in the current project plan; do not invent one.

- CEO reports surviving picks to Bryson. No orders are placed.

## Examples

**Correct:** A candidate is researched, ranked, challenged by the Red Team, and either retained as a paper pick or recorded as cut. CEO sends surviving paper picks to Bryson, who alone decides whether to place a bet.

**Incorrect:** A teammate places an order, reports only winning picks, or describes a model estimate as a guaranteed result.
